BOQ Diff privacy policy

QuantForge – BOQ Diff compares the files you select on your Windows device. The app processes BOQ descriptions, quantities, rates and amounts locally. It does not send those files or comparison results to QuantForge Labs.

Files and reports

The comparison stays in memory during the session. Exporting writes a report to the location you select, using a temporary file in that folder. Reports contain commercial data and source file names. You control how those files are stored, shared and deleted. A cloud-synced destination can sync reports according to your own provider settings.

Accounts and analytics

The app has no account, analytics, advertising or subscription. Microsoft operates Store purchases and distribution under its own privacy terms.

Support

For a product or privacy question, use the publisher support channel shown on the Store listing. Include only information you intend to share. Please keep live tender files and personal details out of public reports.
